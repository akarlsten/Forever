# Beta build 1.60.1.70425 (16 October 2026)

Client 1.60.1.70374 -> 1.60.1.70425: 2,780 changed rows in the sim's tables
(`docs/data-changes/2026-10-16-client-1.60.1.70425.md`). Numbers come from the regenerated store (CDN build plus
raidbots' DBCache.bin hotfixes, so a value can differ from wago's unhotfixed CSV: Frost Blast is 0.271 in the store,
0.343 on wago).

## Unverified: read from tooltips, not rows or logs

| Mechanic | What the sim does |
|---|---|
| Improved Mind Flay | ticks compound, tick n at (1+x)^(n-1); additive steps are the other reading |
| Fire Power | counts Ignite, Fireball/Pyroblast/Flamestrike dots and Improved Scorch as "your Fire effects" |
| Missile Barrage | Arcane Missile hits proc at a quarter, Arcane Wrath and the bolts at half of Arcane Blast's chance |
| Heating Up | a Fire Blast crit grants two stacks |
| Arcane Wrath | +5% of max mana as damage at coefficient 1.0 |
| Frost Blast | counts as a chill for Fingers of Frost (its family flags match Frostbolt's) |
| Elemental Mastery | lasts its full 15 sec; the row's one charge is not spent by a cast |
| Water Shield | the crit trigger counts both harmful and healing crits |
| Dark Inspiration | Mind Blast procs it on cast complete, Mind Flay after its last tick; it refreshes, it doesn't stack |
| Reckoning | blocks (1.5 sec lockout) and crits taken store up to 4 swings; the next main-hand swing, even a miss, releases them all, each a free swing that leaves the timer alone |
| Blade of Hanna | only main-hand auto attacks stack Improving Form |
| Lord Sakrasis' Scepter | time-based 2 PPM on spell damage hits |

## Left as is

- Death Mark (rogue 1316709) is in the client but nothing teaches it (no SkillLineAbility, trait or trigger), so the
  sim leaves it out. Its rows: finisher with a 10 sec cooldown that stores 12% of your damage per combo point and
  deals it after 6 sec.
- Battle Shout, Trueshot Aura, Strength of Earth, Grace of Air, Leader of the Pack and Moonkin Aura are raid-wide now,
  but they stay in `PartyBuffs` (as scope exemptions in `tools/database/buffs_regen_test.go`) so saved settings keep
  working. Moving them to `RaidBuffs` is a proto change.
- Gloves of Holy Might (867): a chance on hit with no rate in the client, so it stays a generated TODO.
- Spell store rows with a `SpellProcsPerMinute` id don't read that table, so a PPM proc the generator emits fires
  as if it were a plain chance. Lord Sakrasis' Scepter is hand-implemented because of this.
